# "Going Dark" Perception Management Cycle Diagram

## Description
A circular/cycle diagram showing how the "Going Dark" narrative functions as a perpetual perception management loop. The cycle demonstrates how intelligence agencies publicly perform impotence to generate legislative pretext for expanded access, while already possessing the capabilities they claim to lack. Supports Section 7 on perception management.

## Placement
