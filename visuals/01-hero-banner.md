# Hero Banner - The Panopticon Paradigm

## Description
A dramatic cover/hero image for the paper that visually encapsulates the thesis: digital privacy is a managed illusion, not a contested right. The image uses the metaphor of a glass house inside a digital panopticon - a person sitting in a transparent structure surrounded by invisible watchers, data streams, and surveillance architecture. The aesthetic is dark, cinematic, and academic.

## Placement
Top of the HTML paper, spanning the full width as a hero/cover banner above the title and abstract.

## Gemini Image Generation Prompt
Render in a glassmorphism aesthetic throughout: use frosted glass panels with backdrop blur effects, semi-transparent layers with visible depth, subtle light borders that catch highlights, soft diffused shadows behind panels, and a layered floating composition with elements at different depth planes. Use translucent fills rather than solid colors for all major surfaces.

A dramatic, cinematic wide-format illustration for an academic paper cover. A solitary figure sits inside a transparent glass house at the center of a vast circular surveillance structure inspired by Jeremy Bentham's panopticon. The glass walls of the house are rendered as frosted glassmorphism panels with backdrop blur, translucent fills, and subtle luminous borders that catch highlights. Data streams, code fragments, and metadata flow across their semi-transparent surfaces, showing that nothing inside is truly hidden. The circular tower surrounding the glass house is dark and monolithic, with hundreds of unseen observation points emitting faint red sensor glows. Digital circuit-board patterns and binary code are subtly embedded into the architecture of the tower walls. The lighting is moody and atmospheric: cold blue light illuminates the glass house from within, while the observation tower remains in shadow with only faint crimson accents. Thin threads of light connect the glass house to the tower, symbolizing constant data exfiltration. The floor beneath the glass house is a circuit board pattern fading into darkness. The overall mood is ominous, clinical, and authoritative. Style: photorealistic digital art with architectural precision. No text or typography in the image. Dark background, high contrast, professional academic journal quality. Aspect ratio 21:9 ultrawide.
