# National Security Letter and Surveillance Metrics Chart

## Description
A hybrid bar/line chart visualizing key surveillance compliance and privacy breach statistics from the Intelligence Community's own oversight reports. Shows the scale of NSL usage, the rising proportion targeting U.S. persons, Section 702 query compliance rates, and documented privacy breach rates. Supports Section 4 on legal architecture as access infrastructure.

## Placement
Section 4: Legal Architecture as Access Infrastructure - alongside the discussion of FISA Section 702, National Security Letters, and oversight limitations.
