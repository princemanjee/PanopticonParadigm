# Digital Panopticon Concept Diagram

## Description
A conceptual diagram mapping the paper's central thesis onto the panopticon model. The observer (state/Intelligence Community) sits at the center with full visibility, surrounded by concentric surveillance layers (hardware, cryptographic, legal, corporate, cloud, perception), while the observed (citizens) occupy the outer ring, believing they have privacy walls that are in fact transparent. This is the paper's core visual metaphor.

## Placement
Section 1: Introduction / Thesis Statement - or as a standalone conceptual figure referenced throughout the paper. Could also serve as a secondary hero image.

Each figure sits in what appears to be a walled cell, but the walls are drawn with dashed or transparent lines, showing they are illusory. Label: "The Observed: Citizens. Style: Architectural diagram meets conceptual art. Clean, symmetrical, academic. Dark and authoritative. Minimal decorative elements.
