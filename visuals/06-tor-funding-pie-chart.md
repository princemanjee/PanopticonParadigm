# Tor Project Funding Sources Pie Chart (2024)

## Description

The visual highlights the structural irony that the world's most prominent "privacy tool" receives its largest single funding source from the U.S. State Department's Bureau of Democracy, Human Rights, and Labor (DRL). This supports Section 7 on perception management and controlled opposition.
