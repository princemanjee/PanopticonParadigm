# PRISM Provider Integration Timeline and Data Access Chart

## Description
An infographic combining a timeline of PRISM provider integration dates with a data visualization showing the scope of access and growth in collection volume. This visual illustrates how major technology companies were systematically integrated into the PRISM program over a five-year period, with dramatic growth in data collection by 2012. Supports Section 5 on corporate integration as structural merger.
