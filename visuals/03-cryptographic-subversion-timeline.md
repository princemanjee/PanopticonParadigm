# Cryptographic Subversion Timeline (1993-2013)

## Description
A horizontal timeline charting the evolution of state cryptographic subversion from overt, public mandates (Clipper Chip) to covert, embedded sabotage (Dual_EC_DRBG, BULLRUN). The visual uses a "going underground" metaphor where the timeline physically descends below a surface line, showing how tactics shifted from visible policy battles to invisible infrastructure compromise. This supports Section 3 of the paper on cryptographic subversion.

## Placement
Section 3: Cryptographic Subversion - spanning the full width of the section, positioned after the introductory paragraphs about the tactical pivot.
